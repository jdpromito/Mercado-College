Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common inquiries about Mercado College.

What is the enrollment process for new students?

Visit the Admissions Office in Tanauan City with your required documents: Form 138/137, Certificate of Good Moral Character, Original PSA Birth Certificate, 2x2 & 1x1 ID pictures (3 copies each), and NCAE/NSAT result. Applicants must also pass the entrance exam, interview, and physical exam at DMMC Industrial Clinic.

What are the campus operating hours?

Administrative offices are open Monday through Friday, from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Are scholarships and financial aid available?

Yes! We offer Institutional Scholarships (Daniel Mercado Scholarship, Entrant, Academic Excellence, Student Financial Assistance) for programs in Elementary/Secondary Education, Biology, Psychology, and Physical Therapy. External grants like Student Leadership are also available. Inquire at the Admissions Office to apply.

Can I request documents through email?

Yes, you may send your document requests or inquiries to registrar@mercadocollege.edu.ph along with your full details.

What programs and courses do you offer?

We offer Basic Education, TESDA courses, and professional Degree Programs in Nursing, Midwifery, MedTech, PT, RadTech, Education, Psychology, and Business Administration.

How do I request a Certificate of Good Moral Character?

Requests can be submitted through the Student Affairs Office or Registrar's Office along with your student clearance.
